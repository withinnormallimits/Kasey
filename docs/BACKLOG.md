# Backlog

Things consciously deferred past v1. Each entry says what it is, why it is not
in v1, and what it would change. Nothing lands here by drift. If something is
here, someone decided it.

---

## Child access to their own record

Kasey v1 is parent-only and single child. The autistic person has no way to
read, correct, or control what is written about them.

`docs/PRODUCT.md` rule 10 already names this as a community objection. A
reviewer raised exactly this against a competitor on Google Play, saying it was
demeaning that there is no option for autistic individuals to use the app
themselves.

We have not decided what the answer looks like, and we should not decide it
before the autistic adult review in rule 10 happens.

Options not yet evaluated:
- a read-only view the child can open
- a per-entry visibility setting
- an age gate

Not a v1 item because it changes the data model and the sharing rules.

## Not in v1

Moved here from `docs/ROADMAP.md` so the two do not drift apart.

- **Multiple children.** Multi-child is where scope creep starts and it is not
  why anyone opens the app.
- **Accounts, sync, or cloud anything.** Changes our HIPAA and FTC posture.
  Requires an explicit recorded decision in `docs/PRODUCT.md` first.
- **Sharing infrastructure beyond a PDF.** A PDF goes anywhere and needs no
  server. Anything more needs infrastructure we cannot sustain.
- **Notifications beyond one appointment-anchored reminder.** No streaks, no
  daily nagging.
- **Anything that predicts or explains rather than counts.** The app counts. It
  does not diagnose and it does not claim causation.

Say no to all of these once and keep saying no.
